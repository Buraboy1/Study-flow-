What is permeability?

Permeability describes how easily fluids can move through connected spaces within a rock.

In petroleum engineering, it matters because it affects how easily reservoir fluids can flow toward a production well.

Think about it

Why would a reservoir rock with higher permeability generally allow fluids to move more easily?

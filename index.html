What is permeability?

Permeability describes how easily fluids can move through the connected spaces within a rock.

In petroleum engineering, permeability is important because it affects how easily oil, gas and water can flow through reservoir rock toward a well.

💡 Remember

Porosity tells us about the amount of pore space.

Permeability tells us about how easily fluid can flow through connected pore spaces.
